# Design an interface

Read the [core](core.md) and its selected design references. Acquire the actual
surface, source behavior, product context, relevant states and platform floor.
Capture renders or screenshots when the task concerns an existing visible surface.
A source-only view is not enough to claim visual improvement.

Apply the core. For unresolved feel or structure, use prototype to build and
exercise the requested experiment; feed observations back into the design.
For open expressive directions that keep converging, use the creative method
identified by the expressive-direction reference to supply alternatives, retaining
the invariant brief. Do not treat a familiar product surface as permission to
invent a new brand or purpose.

Implement only within the task's scope, preserving auth/data and local grammar.
Render the result across meaningful states, viewports and interaction paths;
provide those receipts to the core for assessment. Continue until the named issue
is resolved or the evidence limit is explicit. The core's proposal is not proof
of browser behavior.

Return changes, observed checks and remaining risks, or findings for critique-only
work. Use Augment design when its identity applies. Settle disputed owner choices
through decision-rights without turning taste into a veto.
