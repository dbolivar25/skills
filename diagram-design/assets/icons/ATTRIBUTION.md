# Icon attribution

These assets were relocated from Diagram Design's original inline catalog. Source names and license labels below are retained provenance, not a fresh audit of every upstream logo. Original IDs and geometry are preserved. Added XML namespace declarations make the snippets standalone files.

- [Tabler Icons](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons): catalog identifies MIT.
- [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons): catalog identifies CC0.
- [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon): catalog identifies MIT.
- [log-z/logos](https://github.com/log-z/logos): catalog identifies MIT.

Brand names and logos retain their respective trademarks. Do not treat an icon collection's license label as permission for every brand use. The direct-fetch hop, pentaho, dagster, sas, and stata assets have unresolved license provenance in the original catalog. Resolve that provenance when the requested use requires it; do not present them as verified MIT or CC0 assets.
