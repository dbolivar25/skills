# Writing

Own the requested prose as a faithful and useful work product. Determine the speaker, reader, purpose, genre, destination, and relevant source. Use actual writing examples when speaker matching matters and available host style tools when appropriate. Current user instructions own house style.

## Select drafting or editing

- Draft new prose or update an account of current work: obtain the relevant current material and build a coherent account before writing. For a change account, read [change reconstruction](references/change-account.md); for a PR description, also read [pull-request guidance](references/pull-request.md). Reconstruct the final aggregate delta, responsible regions, intended versus observed behavior, meaningful tradeoffs, and actual validation. Do not silently start a full merge review.
- Supplied prose: preserve meaning, factual claims, uncertainty, argument dependencies, protected quotations, code, names, dates, links, citations, and format requirements. Rewrite at the requested depth. Do not add stronger conclusions or new claims merely to make it sound confident.

Read [prose craft](references/prose-craft.md) for an expression/voice problem, and [strict style](references/strict-style.md) only for an established strict-style contract. When compressing, read [information fidelity](references/information-fidelity.md) if the reader's downstream judgment could lose necessary evidence. Preserve details they need and recoverable source pointers. Disclose a consequential omission when it changes what they can conclude; do not make every short edit carry a loss ledger.

## Write for the real reader

Lead with the point, use concrete language, and connect reasons to consequences. Match natural cadence, useful quirks, and the speaker's level of certainty. Cut generic padding and artificial structure when it contributes nothing. Preserve artistic or expressive choices that fit the work.

Use genre-sensitive craft rather than a universal detector score or banned-word quota. Strict house constraints apply when requested or established, including the user's rule against em dashes in generated copy. A technical description should give reviewers the behavior, mechanism, relevant proof, and remaining decision at the scale the change requires.

## Finish the actual artifact

Check the draft against its sources and the requested meaning, voice, format, and destination. For rendered documents or presentations, inspect the output people will use. Save, post, or send only within the task's authority; drafting alone does not imply an external message.

Finish with usable prose, preserving material evidence and uncertainty. Do not expose a mandatory internal account template unless that format helps the intended reader.
