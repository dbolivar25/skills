# Augment design core

Read the [core/shell contract](../contracts/core-shell.md).

**Inputs:** Surface purpose/branch, supplied brand foundations/tokens/assets, proposed or actual composition/copy, scope/permission constraints, rendered and audit observations when assessing.

Apply supplied Augment identity and the relevant surface floor to the user's
moment. Read [composition law](references/composition.md) and the selected branch:
[documents](references/documents.md), [product UI](references/product-ui.md) or
[marketing](references/marketing.md). Keep each surface's floor and facets distinct.

Use the supplied foundations and token values; missing tokens, official identity
assets or typeface evidence are needs, not permission to invent replacements.
Brand policy includes official SVG identity, Matter SQ, sentence case, no emoji
or exclamation points, and approved customer-name visibility. A task that only
requests assets need not fabricate a complete surface design.

For composition and interaction, use [interface judgment](../interface-design/core.md)
with supplied renders and the brand constraints. For copy, apply the voice method
and [human prose core](../humanize/core.md) in the explicitly required strict mode.
Preserve facts and meaning over style. Read [voice](references/voice.md) as the
brand's prose criteria; execution and file updates stay in the shell.

Return the proposed brand application or assessed findings with reasons tied to
actual supplied content. Audit output establishes only what the checker observes;
a clean audit cannot prove focal point, hierarchy or prose quality. No rendered
quality claim is established without supplied renders of relevant pages/states.
The shell owns asset copying, building, rendering and running the audit.
