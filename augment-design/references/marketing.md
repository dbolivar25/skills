# Branch: marketing

Web pages, hero surfaces, brand panels, launch and campaign artwork.

## Moment

Someone with no obligation to keep reading arrives, gives the surface a second,
and decides whether Augment is serious.

## Floor

A clear claim, legible type, a working path forward, and one image or field
composed on purpose. Below the floor is a page of assertions with a gradient
behind them.

## Facets

Confident, material, and quietly alive. It should read as serious
infrastructure with some warmth in it.

## Expressive license

Atmosphere and artwork are freely available in this branch and nowhere else, and
the license comes with conditions. Before you use swirl, glow, grain, or an
artwork hue, answer three questions:

1. What is this surface's one job?
2. What does the expression add that type and spacing could not?
3. At what scale does it work? Is it a field, or a decoration sitting on chrome?

Cut it if the answer to the second question is visual interest, and cut it if
the answer to the third is small. Expression works at the scale of a field, and
turns into noise on chrome.

## Material

Swirl and glow belong on large fields and cropped hero artwork, away from routine
chrome. Grain belongs on large surfaces: dark sections, brand panels, page
canvases. Keep it off buttons and avatars. Pair grain with a brand glow so the
glow does not band. Motion, where you use it, is a slow drift that respects
reduced motion. `readme.md` holds the mechanics.

## What holds anyway

Everything in `composition.md`. A hero with three elements at equal weight has no
focal point, and atmosphere will not fix that. Type leads, and claims earn their
keep, which `references/voice.md` covers.
