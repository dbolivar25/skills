# Branch: documents

Reports, account briefs, business cases, memos, anything exported to PDF or read
as a page instead of operated as an interface.

## Moment

Someone with authority reads this once, on a screen or on paper, and decides
whether to act. They did not ask for it, and they will not scroll back.

## Floor

The credible baseline is an internal analyst brief. The reader can tell what is
claimed, where each claim came from, and what it costs to act. Below the floor is
a page whose facts cannot be traced. Treat the floor as a minimum, and aim higher.

## Facets

Calm, exact, editorial, source aware. A document earns attention by being worth
reading, and signalling that it should be read counts for nothing.

## What this branch narrows

A document claims one chromatic role, and only one: either signal, meaning the
state or number the reader must notice, or atmosphere, as a single large brand
field. Not both on the same page, and not on every page. Identity appears
wherever the logo appears, and artwork stays out of documents.

That leaves page chrome neutral. Rules, dividers, section numbers, arrows, and
labels take `--neutral-*` or ink, which is tighter than `composition.md` asks
for and accounts for most of the difference between a document and a marketing
page.

Since `composition.md` reserves frames for objects, and most of a document is
prose, most of a document sits flat on the page. Sources, metrics, and
properties are facts, so give them aligned label and value rows separated by
hairline rules, or a plain labelled column.

Leave out the interface furniture. Buttons, pills, tabs, and toolbars imply an
action the reader cannot take, and a static export has no controls anyway.

## Composition

Rather than working from a template, compose the page from the moment and let
the shape of the argument set the shape of the page. Two things hold whatever
shape you land on: each page has one focal point, usually the evidence rather
than the heading, and type does the work of structure, so size, weight, and
spacing separate sections where boxes would not.

## Practical floors

- 12pt minimum body text in print, 16px minimum on screen.
- Long-form text inside the measure tokens, 44 to 72ch.
- A source, date, or record for every number the reader could question.
- Every page rendered and read at full size before delivery.
