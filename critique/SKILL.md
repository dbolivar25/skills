name: critique
description: Use when a proposed understanding, plan, design, or result needs deliberate falsification against its purpose and constraints. Load it to return evidence-backed weaknesses and coverage limits while leaving acceptance and execution with the caller.
---

# Critique a candidate

The caller supplies purpose, governing constraints, candidate, relevant evidence,
requested axes and the decision the critique should inform. Inspect those sources
before judging. Return one supported assessment; do not rewrite the candidate,
change owner decisions, schedule a team, or grant acceptance.

## Establish the comparison

Separate what the candidate must accomplish from what its author claims. Identify
material changes in premises since it was produced. Resolve accessible facts;
name missing purpose, evidence or decision criteria rather than substituting taste.
Reuse the caller's domain-specific axes. For each axis define what would falsify
adequacy and where that evidence could be found.

## Attempt falsification

Trace consequential claims to their source or observed mechanism. Look for
omissions, unsupported commitments, contradictions, excluded viable alternatives,
and specificity or machinery the purpose does not require. Test counterexamples
and attempt to disprove suspected defects before retaining them. A preference
without a consequence is advice, not a blocker.

Account for every requested axis as checked or limited. Distinguish a verified
conflict from a plausible risk and from evidence too weak to judge. Do not turn
an absent test into a demonstrated product failure or a green check into proof
of a broader outcome.

## Return the assessment

For each retained finding give the affected claim or location, governing
criterion, evidence, mechanism, consequence and correction direction or owner
question. Group one cause once. Return an overall assessment with checked scope,
unchecked axes and confidence grounded in the sources. Use the caller's result
schema when supplied; otherwise use Supported, At risk, Conflicts, or Insufficient
evidence. Supported means no material defect found in the checked scope.

Independence is an execution property. When independent critique is requested,
use an agent that did not author the candidate and provide the necessary source
context. If unavailable, report that limit; a self-check cannot satisfy an
independent acceptance gate. Ordinary critique does not require a new agent.
The caller owns scheduling, acceptance, further work and any publication.
