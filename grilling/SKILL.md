# Grilling

Read the [core](core.md). Establish the object and decision, inspect accessible
sources, and give the core the current answers and evidence. Ask its complete
frontier as one numbered round with titles, context and recommendations.
Wait for answers before recomputing dependent questions; do not treat elapsed
time as settlement. Continue acquiring independent facts while awaiting answers.

Keep discoverable facts with the agent and consequential choices with the owner.
When requested with docs, use domain-modeling to persist settled language and
qualifying decisions as the conversation progresses. Do not write open material
as settled or create documents outside that branch.

When the frontier is empty or blocked on named evidence, present decisions,
tensions and remaining gaps, then ask the user to confirm shared understanding.
End at that confirmation; implementation requires its own direction. For an
objection that could block an owner choice, use decision-rights and preserve its
settlement instead of turning the interview into repeated argument.
