# Faithful derivation

Read the [core](core.md), glossary and contract. Establish whether the task is
design, review, expert elicitation or implementation handoff. Acquire the source,
graph, artifact, scope and evidence needed for that mode from the actual system;
keep conceptual designs distinct from observed runtime behavior.

Apply the core. Resolve its obtainable evidence needs with the appropriate source
capabilities. For expert knowledge, use [the interview guide](guides/expert-interview.md),
ask concrete dependency-ready questions and feed answers into the core's spec.
Do not turn the entire question bank into a mandatory interview.

For authorized implementation, follow [the handoff guide](guides/implementation-handoff.md)
and the actual repository or product operations. Supply the resulting code,
contracts and receipts back to the core to check preservation. Augment workflow
operations remain with their product owner; a derivation spec does not grant
product access or permission for its effects.

Return the complete design or supported review, integrating supporting judgments
and their limits. Separate candidate generation from publication. Perform only
the requested lifecycle/effect and report the actual observed stage. An accepted
workflow or provider request is not proof of faithful visible output or delivery.
