# Faithful derivation

The work product is the product; the workflow is the means. Design backward from
what the output must get right to the judgments, raw state, and transformations
that make it possible. Execute forward only when that derivation can protect the
claims, actions, omissions, tone, recommendations, and publication behavior.

Raw state is canonical. Shared retrieval infrastructure may be neutral; semantic
observations are purpose- and scope-bound until a promotion contract establishes
meaning, fidelity, confidence, invalidation, consumers, and ownership. Structured input does not establish relevance or fidelity. A universal summary
or fixed pipeline cannot substitute for those decisions.

## Select the work

Read the [vocabulary](GLOSSARY.md) and [derivation contract](CONTRACT.md), then the
matching guide. The contract owns common obligations; the guides apply them,
references explain the judgments, and templates record the result.

| Task | Guide | Required result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New work product or major redesign | [Design](guides/design-new-work-product-workflow.md) | A derivation spec, not a generic implementation plan |
| Existing proposed or running workflow | [Review](guides/review-existing-workflow.md) | Faithfulness gaps and a supported PASS / REVISE / REPLAN judgment |
| Domain-expert knowledge or an ambiguous product idea | [Interview](guides/expert-interview.md) | Concrete answers translated into a spec for expert review |
| Accepted design becoming code, prompts, tools, or a product workflow | [Implementation](guides/implementation-handoff.md) | The accepted derivation preserved through actual implementation boundaries |

Read a referenced method before its governed decision, reusing content already
loaded. An isolated worker needs the applicable contract, guide, and reference
content in its own context; a parent pointer does not provide that context.

## Preserve what downstream judgment needs

Compression is invalid when a required later judgment can no longer be made.
Exact commitments, owners, dates, stakeholder distinctions, and scope must survive
until their consumers are finished with them. Recoverable pointers, classification,
aggregation, decay, and dropping are different choices with different costs.

Separate candidate generation from permission to show, send, persist, recommend,
or act. Structured confidence must change rendering or omission; fluent prose and
one confidence score do not establish support. Evaluate retrieval, judgments, and
edges as well as final output, then optimize the coherent quality path.

A design or interview uses the [derivation spec](templates/derivation-spec.md);
a review uses the [review report](templates/review-report.md). Local product,
repository, and execution mechanics come from their actual owners. This skill
owns faithful derivation, not a cache of those systems' schemas.

## Compose the supporting modules

This skill owns the complete work-product design and its acceptance. Use the
supporting modules at the level the current task can actually supply:

- When designing or interviewing, read the owned preservation and support
  references linked by the guide to define required fidelity and support policy.
  Do not pretend a concrete output or source assessment already exists.
- When reviewing an actual source-to-output transformation, use
  [information-preservation](../information-preservation/SKILL.md) with the source,
  candidate representation and downstream judgments.
- When assessing concrete claims, use [claim-support](../claim-support/SKILL.md)
  with their sources, scope and consequence; preserve its uncertainty in the
  review rather than treating an intended support policy as observed support.
- Use [evaluation-design](../evaluation-design/SKILL.md) with the accepted quality
  obligations and proposed system to obtain the evaluation plan. Executed quality
  and publication decisions still need their own evidence.

A supporting result does not accept the whole derivation. Integrate it with the
DAG, raw-state semantics, publication policy and consumer obligations owned here.
