# Refactoring proposal

The shared source-intent investigation is independently accepted for planning. That acceptance does not prove a rewrite. This proposal is grounded in the originals and the owner's current instructions: remove STE and Herdr, preserve the creative split, recover essential details before changing their representation, and carry the full authorized refactor through verification.

## Choice of suite shape

Three shapes were considered:

1. **Merge by topic.** Combining review/evidence, design/prototyping, or prose/agent writing would reduce entry points. It would also make one trigger activate multiple distinct methods and make ownership less clear. The source investigation supplies counterexamples: a telemetry count is not a causal verdict; restoring a voice is not specifying an agent's process; a diagram can explain an answer without discovering one. Reject this topology.
2. **Keep all text and repair only links.** This preserves material but leaves contradictory teaching and duplicated operating policy in active use. It cannot address the confirmed drift. Reject as insufficient.
3. **Keep distinct methods, repair their seams, and disclose complete branch work at its decision point.** Retire owner-rejected packages. Preserve common judgment, mandatory coverage, and discriminating examples. Consolidate authoritative explanations only where consumers can reliably reach them. Choose this shape.

The result is not judged by the number of files or words. Unchanged packages are deliberate retain decisions, not omitted work.

## Retain decisions

No content rewrite is currently justified for engineering-restraint, domain-modeling, coding-standards, decision-rights, steward, grafana-evidence, write-custom-lint, augment-workflows, show-me, or humanize. These were read and compared with neighbors. Their distinct methods and detail carry present value. Some gain catalog coverage or cleaner callers without needing a rewritten body.

Keep diagnosing-bugs' signal-first discipline and concrete reproduction strategies. Its absolutist claims may deserve a focused later investigation, but this pass will not silently relax the owner's original method merely because production investigation can be difficult.

Keep codebase-design's glossary and design-diversity branch; change only the demonstrated testing-preservation seam. Keep designing-human-interfaces' mechanics, worked examples, and creative caller; it gains only a clear relationship between generic examples and an applicable design system if the current wording leaves that precedence unclear.

## Completion

Each slice requires an original-to-new meaning check and a concrete contrast that could reveal lost behavior. Structural tests check document reachability, metadata, and corpus consistency; they do not establish model behavior. Run the existing relevant executable tests when their consumers change. Use fresh execution probes for representative changed guidance, with outcomes reported separately from static checks. Independent review must challenge request fit and preservation before completion.

The final local delivery includes the branch commit, per-skill dispositions, preservation map, observed checks and limits. No push, merge, Amp publication, product execution is part of this refactor.
