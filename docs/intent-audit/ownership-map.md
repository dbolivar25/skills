## Writing and derivation

Writing-for-agents is rewritten around author decisions rather than preserving
its former paragraph arrangement. Context pointers and invocation contracts,
context versus cognitive load, three hierarchy positions, disclosure reliability,
co-location versus duplication, live sprawl, real-context sequence splits,
completion clarity versus demand, legwork, leading-word examples, exact guardrails,
model-relative no-op testing, authoritative environment/cache, scripts versus
judgment, and sediment all remain. The new structure makes their interventions
explicit without imposing a universal output template.

Faithful-derivation's main now owns posture and routing. `CONTRACT.md` owns the
shared derivation obligations formerly repeated in main. Each guide applies that
contract to its task, retaining every task-specific completion obligation.
Glossary, explanatory references, templates, concrete examples and fields are
unchanged. Internally iterative judgment and speculative generation remain
compatible with a dependency DAG; the common method is not a fixed runtime chain.
