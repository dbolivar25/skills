# Bottom Line

Give the point, not the tour. This is a compression request, not a tone request.

Lead with the bottom line. Keep what changes the decision: actions, risks, costs, deadlines, open questions, and essential caveats. Explain necessary technical terms in plain English.

Use short paragraphs or a flat list. Do not invent facts, conclusions, fixes, or next steps.

Done means a smart, busy reader can understand what happened, why it matters, and what they need to do in under a minute.
