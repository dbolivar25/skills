# Strict house-style branch

Strict mode is an explicit editorial constraint, not the default definition of
human writing. Apply it only after the main skill's meaning and voice invariants
are pinned.

## Directness

- Cut throat-clearing, signposting, emphasis crutches, and meta-commentary.
- State the claim instead of staging a reveal or rhetorical setup.
- Rewrite mechanical binary contrasts and negative listings into the positive
  claim unless the contrast carries real logic.
- Cut vague declarations of importance and replace them with the specific
  consequence already present in the source.

## Actors and specificity

- Prefer active voice and name the responsible actor.
- Replace false agency with what a person, team, buyer, or system actually did.
- Replace abstract collectives such as "people" with the known audience or
  actor when the source supplies one.
- Remove lazy extremes when the source does not justify them.

Do not invent an actor or convert an unknown into a specific. Passive voice is
correct when responsibility is genuinely unknown, irrelevant, or protected.

## Density

- Remove filler intensifiers, softeners, and adverbs that add no meaning.
- Use one precise term per concept instead of synonym cycling.
- Delete sentences that only repeat the heading, announce the section, or
  produce a generic conclusion.
- Trust the reader with direct facts rather than reassurance or hand-holding.

Keep adverbs, hedges, repetition, and explanation when they carry scope,
certainty, sequence, safety, or voice.

## Rhythm

- Remove em and en dashes unless an explicit user constraint or authoritative
  voice sample uses them.
- Break padded rule-of-three lists; use the natural number of items.
- Avoid runs of dramatic fragments and manufactured punchlines.
- Vary sentence and paragraph endings without forcing visible variety.
- Prefer sentence-case headings and plain formatting unless the artifact has a
  stronger design system.

## Gate

Score the finished prose from 1 to 10 on:

| Dimension | Test |
| --- | --- |
| Directness | Does it state claims rather than announce or stage them? |
| Rhythm | Does the cadence fit the speaker without a repeated formula? |
| Trust | Does it respect the reader without reassurance or performance? |
| Authenticity | Does it sound like this speaker rather than a style template? |
| Density | Does every sentence carry meaning, voice, or necessary structure? |

Below 35 out of 50 requires another pass. A passing score cannot excuse meaning
drift, fabrication, lost uncertainty, or violation of the artifact's real
conventions.
