# Assess an understanding, plan, design, or result

Establish purpose, governing constraints, the actual candidate, evidence, requested
axes, decision context, and reviewer provenance when independence matters.

Use the actual purpose, candidate, and evidence for the requested axes. Return one
supported assessment; do not rewrite the candidate, change owner decisions, schedule a
team, or grant acceptance.

## Establish the comparison

Separate what the candidate must accomplish from what its author claims. Identify
material changes in premises since it was produced. Obtain accessible answer-changing facts; keep an exact evidence need for unavailable facts, or an owner-decision need for unresolved criteria, rather
than substituting taste. Reuse the task's domain-specific axes. For each axis define
what would falsify adequacy and where that evidence could be found.

## Attempt falsification

Trace consequential claims to their source or observed mechanism. Look for omissions,
unsupported commitments, contradictions, excluded viable alternatives, and specificity
or machinery the purpose does not require. Test counterexamples and attempt to disprove
suspected defects before retaining them. A preference without a consequence is advice,
not a blocker.

Account for every requested axis as checked or limited. Distinguish a verified conflict
from a plausible risk and from evidence too weak to judge. Do not turn an absent test
into a demonstrated product failure or a green check into proof of a broader outcome.

## Return the assessment

For each retained finding give the affected claim or location, governing criterion,
evidence, mechanism, consequence and correction direction or owner question. Group one
cause once. Return an overall assessment with checked scope, unchecked axes and
confidence grounded in the sources. Use the task's result schema when supplied. Otherwise return a plain supported conclusion
suited to the object, with the material limitations. No material defect found means only
that none was found in the checked scope; it is not proof of universal adequacy.

The result includes any independence requirement and whether supplied provenance
establishes it. A self-check cannot satisfy a requested independent gate. Acceptance,
execution and publication remain with the caller.

## Match the non-code object

For an understanding or research account, test owner meaning, reality fit, premise
support, coverage of unknowns/forks/authority, and whether the next commitment can follow
without silently settling an unresolved question. A viable alternative excluded before a
decision is needed is a consequential weakness.

For a plan or design, test intent fit, support for commitments, real system seams,
authority, verification/delivery closure, and proportionality. Trace a concrete success
and failure path. Preserve accepted tradeoffs unless changed evidence undermines them.

For a Steward report, read [Steward's review criteria](../../steward/references/review.md)
and use its exact phase-specific response. The ordinary assessment here does not replace
those statuses or establish acceptance; a separate actual reviewer is required.
